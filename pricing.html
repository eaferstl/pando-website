How billing works

Billing is based on the second-highest weekly protected-node count recorded in a calendar month, not the single monthly peak, and weeks with no heartbeat count as zero, so your worst week is free and one spike never inflates your bill. Billed monthly, no proration. Only protected nodes are billed. Usage is measured via a weekly heartbeat from each protected node, with no reconciliation required. Need a different plan? Contact us and we'll move you over.

Growth is issued by hand against an invoice, so there is no billing toggle to flip. Talk to us and we will set it up.
