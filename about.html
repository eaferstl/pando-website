About Pando

Creating innovative technology to enhance security across diverse computing environments

Mission

Pando is dedicated to advancing security and reliability across diverse computing infrastructures. We're building innovative solutions to address fundamental challenges in distributed systems, with a focus on speed, simplicity, and confidentiality for organizations operating across diverse computing environments.

Through rigorous research and development, we're working to establish new standards for secure computing that are both theoretically sound and practically applicable to real-world infrastructure challenges.

Foundation

Pando brings a vast understanding of distributed systems, cryptography, and infrastructure security. Our work is inspired by both academic research and quantum systems.

We understand the real-world constraints and challenges faced by organizations managing compute infrastructure—from traditional cloud deployments to edge computing and hybrid environments. This practical perspective informs our development approach and ensures our innovations address genuine needs.

The Problem We're Addressing

Traditional computing infrastructure faces increasingly complex security and reliability challenges. As systems become more distributed and workloads more sensitive, the limitations of current approaches become more apparent:

Runtime Security

Efficiently protecting your data and logic where encryption can't.

Trustless and Verifiable

Ensuring computational integrity across diverse, potentially untrusted infrastructure environments.

Fault Tolerance

Maintaining security reliability and consistency even when individual components fail or behave unexpectedly.

Endless Scalability

Meeting growing computational demands without compromising security guarantees or operational efficiency.

Platform Diversity

Supporting secure operations across heterogeneous infrastructure from cloud to edge to on-premises deployments.

Security Principles

Our approach to security and reliability is built on fundamental principles:

Platform Independence

No reliance on specialized hardware or trusted execution environments. Our software-based approach enables deployment across diverse infrastructure while maintaining security properties through software techniques.

Multi-Layered Defense

Security derives from multiple independent mechanisms working in concert. This layered approach ensures that compromise of any single element doesn't undermine overall protection.

Active Attack Resistance

Designed to detect and respond to common attack vectors including debugging attempts, memory inspection, code modification, and timing analysis. The system actively monitors execution to identify anomalous conditions.

Validated Performance

Security properties and performance characteristics are continuously validated through rigorous testing, attack simulations, and benchmark analysis. We optimize for real-world deployment while maintaining security guarantees through measurable, verified results.

Scalability by Design

Solutions architected to scale across diverse infrastructure environments, from edge computing devices to large-scale cloud deployments, without compromising security guarantees.
