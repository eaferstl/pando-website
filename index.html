The Reality

Hardware isn't safe.

TEEs and secure enclaves have been compromised. Trusting silicon is a bet against determined attackers.

Networks aren't private.

Traffic analysis, side channels, and infrastructure compromise mean your data's path is exposed.

Runtime is exposed.

Memory inspection, debugging interfaces, and runtime analysis can extract logic and data during execution.

What's New

Pando makes zero assumptions about trust. Our approach treats every layer—from hardware to network to runtime—as potentially hostile. Through software-based techniques inspired by quantum systems, we create protection that works on any infrastructure without relying on "trusted" components. The result: verifiable security across cloud, edge, and hybrid environments where confidentiality and integrity don't depend on trust.
