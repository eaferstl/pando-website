The Challenge

Security Concerns

Current compute infrasttructure faces a plethora of vulnerabilities. Even enhanced security layers, including trusted hardware, is subject to runtime attacks.

Reliability Requirements

Organizations demand consistent, fault-tolerant processing that maintains performance under diverse conditions and unexpected challenges.

Scalability Challenges

Modern applications need solutions that efficiently scale across traditional cloud, edge, and hybrid infrastructure without compromising security.

Our Approach

We're creating innovative approaches to these fundamental challenges in compute security. Our work focuses on developing solutions that reliability and efficiency across diverse environments while disrupting the need for trust in the first place. By combining rigorous research with practical considerations, we're working to advance the state of the art in secure computing. Our approach emphasizes platform-agnostic solutions applicable to traditional cloud, edge, and hybrid deployments.
